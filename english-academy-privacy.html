English Learning Academy – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Local Growth Studio provides English Learning Academy, also presented within the app as Little English Adventure, as an educational application designed for children and families.

Information collected

The current version does not ask children or parents to create an account or provide names, email addresses, phone numbers, photographs, or location information. It does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs. The app itself does not transmit learning progress or other personal information to our servers.

Learning progress stored on the device

The app saves completed lessons, stars, practice progress, and language preferences locally on the device so the learner can continue later. This information is not sent to Local Growth Studio. Removing the app or clearing its storage may erase this progress.

Speech and device services

The app uses the device's text-to-speech service to read learning content aloud. Depending on the speech engine installed and its settings, that service may process text using its own technology and privacy practices. The app does not request microphone access or record children's voices.

Children's privacy

We designed the current version to avoid requesting personal details from children. It has no in-app messaging, user profiles, behavioral advertising, or third-party ad integrations. A parent-only learning control is available within the app.

Sharing and third parties

We do not sell or share children's personal information with advertisers. The current app does not integrate advertising or analytics services. The device's operating system, app store, and text-to-speech provider may separately process information under their own policies.

Changes

If future versions introduce features such as advertising, online services, or new data processing, we will review and update this policy and the app's Google Play disclosures before release.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Local Growth Studio through our Local Growth Facebook page or visit alocalgrowth.org.
